Manufacturing Data Platform: OEE, Machine Health & Predictive Maintenance

Deliverables for a precision machining shop, from integrated data to a live, embedded predictive-maintenance model.

Six deliverables trace the work end to end: a diagnosis of where OEE is lost and what drives unplanned downtime, a machine learning model that predicts each machine's time to its next failure, that prediction delivered inside the existing maintenance system, and the MLOps monitoring that keeps it honest over time. Full detail is in the README.
